# Ocharo Marketing

*Last updated: 2026-09-27*

Maftuna's marketing desk: turn reviewed product inputs into cards and channel-specific copy, plan publication, and inspect real response evidence.

## Product direction

Build for Ocharo first. This temporary venture is independent of the moving Brand Workspace repository. A later merge or independent product decision remains open. Reusable brokers/components are kept separate locally and extracted after the product proves their boundary.

## Working surfaces

| Surface | Job |
|---|---|
| Today | See the next useful action, upcoming work, review needs, and channel health |
| Posts | Write/edit localized copy, select cards/media, inspect readiness, approve exact revisions |
| Products & Media | Reuse existing product cutouts, import source facts, preserve provenance and draft claims |
| Atlas | Compare T01/T02 candidates and later template families; export the exact generated cards |
| Calendar | Plan per-channel releases in Asia/Tashkent; distinguish plans from executable schedules |
| Results | Show observed metrics with channel, time range, freshness, and unavailable states |
| Connections | Explain Instagram/Telegram/X publishing, WhatsApp messaging, and AI provider setup |

## Content creation

Support short product cards, real colour/design choices, wearing-model stills, fabric/work details, work-in-progress ideas, and later motion. Front views and restrained readable typography remain the starting visual rules. Caption assistants propose facts; source records and the operator approve them. Text scanning extracts suggestions and readiness issues from pasted source text. Image scanning/OCR is a separately attributable provider operation.

## Integration priorities

Instagram and Telegram serve the visual product/public-channel workflow. WhatsApp supports customer messaging with recipients/templates and permission requirements; it is not a feed publisher. X is an optional public posting channel. OpenAI and Google Gemini supply image generation/editing through neutral brokers. Additional candidates are Pinterest for discovery, Facebook for reuse of Meta setup, Google Drive for source assets, and link/UTM tracking for qualified enquiries; their priority depends on actual use and provider access.

## Success evidence

Useful measures are preparation time, corrections per approved card, reliable publication receipts, saves/enquiries when available, and attributed qualified enquiries. Never display fabricated reach, followers, trends, or revenue. Provider configuration is not a successful connection test; a queued action is not a published post.
