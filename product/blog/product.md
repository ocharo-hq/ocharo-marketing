# Ocharo Blog — Product

*Last updated: 2026-09-28*

The Ocharo Blog: stories about clothes, curiosity and everyday life, written and scheduled by Maftuna in an Uzbek editor and published in the approved journal design.

## Surfaces

| Surface | Job |
|---|---|
| Hikoyalar | All stories with status, topic, publication time, reading time |
| Hikoya | Structured editor, live preview, publish now / schedule / unpublish / archive, history |
| Bo‘limlar | Topics (site navigation) |
| Sayt | Launch switch (noindex), base URL, texts, footer links, home imagery, static export |
| Public site | Home, archive, topics, stories, RSS, sitemap |

## Rules

- No invented interviews, testimonials, prices or stock claims in stories (inherited editorial rule); catalogue pieces show catalogue facts only.
- Sources must be https links; sections cite sources by number.
- Publishing requires excerpt, introduction and at least one section; a cover needs alt text and a caption.
