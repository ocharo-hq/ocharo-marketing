# Ocharo Blog — media mix for international readers and buyers

> Historical research snapshot. Repository paths, line references and proposals describe the original inspection; current ownership and entrypoints are in [Blog adoption](../../../../../../architecture/blog-adoption.md).

*Last updated: 2026-09-28 10:13 AM*

## Status and recommendation

- Research and editorial proposal. No site, build, account, mailing list, or published content was changed.
- Use the Blog as the durable home for verified reading and guides. Give Instagram discovery, current stockist information, and the existing product catalogue their own jobs.
- Build around **one verified story or product evidence packet**, then choose one or two derivatives that answer different reader questions. Do not turn every article into a Reel, carousel, and email by default.
- Preserve the local sales priority: the [current planning proposal](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../fam/mft-10x-ws/context/domains/external/work/ventures/ocharo/och-planning.md) centres merchants and an Instagram catalogue. It records a three-post weekly mix as **unapproved**, not an existing mandate. An Ocharo media pilot should occupy an agreed slot or operate below that workload.
- *Where/when* means clothing for a place, activity, or date. A verified stockist link can follow the advice when the reader wants a piece. An overseas partner also needs a concise product and capacity brief.

## What Ocharo has now

The [current business plan](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../fam/mft-10x-ws/context/domains/external/work/ventures/ocharo/och-planning.md) records a v22 **product/application catalogue** in Uzbek, Russian, and English, with 29 priced models and approved imagery. A current piece-by-size stock count remains open. The separate [`och-catalogue.md`](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../fam/mft-10x-ws/context/domains/external/work/ventures/ocharo/och-catalogue.md) is a **cloth, motif, and colourway register**; it cannot answer whether a finished garment can be bought today. The proposed Instagram grid is a **living public discovery surface**; the proposed private merchant channel would carry wholesale information, if Maftuna agrees. These are different from a readable Blog article.

The [Ocharo Blog preview](../../och-journal.md) has thirty complete English draft articles across Pieces, Style, and Cloth & craft. Its 35 pages are static, marked `noindex,nofollow`, and have no mailing list or analytics. The drafts provide a reading library; they are not thirty verified product announcements or thirty automatic social posts. Public release, stockist details, language review, and account operation remain separate decisions.

The latest [planning proposal](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../fam/mft-10x-ws/context/domains/external/work/ventures/ocharo/och-planning.md) supersedes the older export-first [channels study](../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../../fam/mft-10x-ws/context/domains/external/work/ventures/ocharo/och-channels.md) where they conflict. Its public-caption sequence is Uzbek first, Russian second, and a short English line; that sequence is still a proposal. International use has two distinct paths: a traveller who can find a named, consenting stockist in Uzbekistan, and an overseas shop or partner who needs a credible assortment and an inquiry route. Neither path should imply online checkout or confirmed export fulfilment.

**Inference from examples:** the useful pattern is *different jobs for different surfaces*. Lenny’s entry page helps discovery; TOAST separates lasting garment explanation from dated event context; Sew Liberated separates dressing ideas from fit answers. TOAST's Happenings is **an events roundup, not an outfit guide**. It supports dating and linking a relevant occasion, while the dressing recommendation must be Ocharo's own work. None of these pages proves that a particular format will engage Ocharo’s audience. We did not compare like counts, comment counts, follower totals, or private conversion data.

## Format jobs

| Format | Reader job | Minimum evidence and asset | Where it should lead | Relative effort |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Editorial article** | Understand one question, story, craft point, or decision in depth | Sourced factual claims; inspected real garment if about Ocharo; approved attribution | Related story, outfit guide, or relevant product guide | Medium; durable |
| **Instagram carousel** | Notice one comparison in a few frames | Real photographs or clearly labelled diagrams; concise bilingual/trilingual copy reviewed for meaning | Article for explanation or product guide for facts | Low to medium once assets exist |
| **Instagram Reel** | See movement, a change of layers, or an actual process | Real footage, permission to film people/venue, accurate captions; generated imagery labelled if used as illustration | Where/when page, relevant guide, or inquiry route | Medium to high; skip if motion proves nothing |
| **Email digest** | Revisit a small set of already published useful items | Working opt-in and sending process, stable links, updated dated claims | Two or three owned pages with distinct purposes | Low marginal effort; high setup/operations burden |
| **Visual guide** | Help someone dress for a place, activity, or date at a glance | Real outfit examples, labelled context, legible mobile layout; verify any claimed event/date | Full explanation, then an optional verified stockist link | Medium upfront; maintenance varies |
| **Product/buyer guide** | Assess one model or an assortment without guessing | Exact model ID, real front/back/detail views, verified sizes, approved prices, current stock or explicit availability uncertainty; private terms only in a private brief | Relevant seller, merchant inquiry, or existing v22 catalogue | Medium upfront; factual maintenance is the main cost |

**Distribution boundary:** the public product guide can explain a model and its one-piece facts when approved. The v22 product/application catalogue remains the authoritative batch of priced model records. The pattern register documents cloth/motif/colourway IDs. A buyer line sheet or private brief can be generated from confirmed product records and terms; the Blog should link to that information rather than maintain a second price list. Instagram remains a visual entrance, not the sole canonical product record. [CBI](https://www.cbi.eu/market-information/apparel/finding-buyers) explicitly recommends consistent photos with multiple angles and details; it does not confirm any Ocharo price or commercial term.

## Four user vectors

These are editorial routes and reader questions, not new app modules. Each row names one primary surface; derivatives are conditional on actual evidence.

| Vector | Reader question | Primary surface | Useful derivative | Accuracy or production gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Culture** | What am I seeing, and whose work or tradition is being described? | Sourced editorial article or illustrated glossary | Carousel comparing a few visible details; short Reel only when real cloth/process footage adds information; digest excerpt | Name the specific object/place/source. Do not claim an individual Ocharo cloth is made by a method merely because a museum explains that method. |
| **Where/when** | What could I wear for this place, activity, or date? | Outfit article or visual guide comparing a few plausible combinations | Occasion carousel; real Reel showing movement or a change of layer; digest link when the advice remains current | Show real garments, label suggestions as suggestions, verify any named event, dress code, weather, or date before claiming it. |
| **Ocharo news** | What has changed that is worth my attention? | Short dated update with the actual decision, event, collaboration, or newly verified product | One photograph or Reel of what changed; digest roundup | Publish only actual milestones and approved names. Do not manufacture weekly news to fill a calendar. |
| **Catalogue** | Which finished piece is this, and what can I ask for? | v22 product record / current product guide; private buyer brief for wholesale specifics | Detail carousel; real movement Reel; optional design article when one documented decision is genuinely interesting | Stock count open; refresh availability before a time-sensitive statement. Keep public and private prices separate. |

### International distribution by path

- **Visitor in Uzbekistan:** a place/activity/date article helps decide what to wear. A separate verified stockist link helps find a piece. If no stockist has agreed to be named, use the existing inquiry channel without inventing an address.
- **Overseas shop or partner:** send a short direct introduction to the brand plus a selected, current product brief: real photographs, model identifiers, range, verified capacity/lead time, and a way to discuss terms. A Blog story can establish taste or provenance but cannot answer an order question alone. [CBI’s buyer guidance](https://www.cbi.eu/market-information/apparel/doing-business) explicitly favours a tailored presentation and accurate capability claims.
- **Existing merchants:** if the proposed private channel is approved, send new model facts, available units, and reorder details there. Public editorial can help their customers understand a design; it should not expose merchant terms or replace fulfilment communication.

## One evidence packet, distinct outputs

**Illustrative scenario:** choose one existing nimcha model after its ID, actual piece, images, material/care information, one-piece price, and current availability are checked. No such checks were performed for this report; the sequence is a production recipe, not a factual claim about a named model.

1. **Source packet:** record model ID and version; front/back/detail photographs and rights; actual measurement method; approved description; material/care label; who can attribute a design decision; stock count and check time; named stockist permission if relevant; Uzbek/Russian/English text reviewed for meaning.
2. **Article:** answer a question such as *What does the diagonal opening change when the layer is worn?* Show the opening from two views and the approved explanation of why it exists. The article stays useful after the individual unit sells.
3. **Carousel:** make the comparison visible: full shape → closed front → opening detail → how it sits over a plain base → a concise model-identification slide. Link to the article if the caption cannot explain the choice adequately. This is visual reading, not a paragraph copied into panels.
4. **Reel, only if filmed:** show the opening in motion or the wearer fastening it. Let the movement answer something the stills cannot. A generated cloth morph is a design illustration and must not stand in for evidence of the physical garment.
5. **Product guide:** supply the current size/measurement facts, actual views, approved price, and inquiry route; label availability with its check time or leave it open. A partner brief adds verified capacity and terms privately.
6. **Email digest, once available:** one paragraph on the design question, one article link, and one current product or location link. Do not republish the whole article into an inbox or imply stock remains unchanged since the article was written.

If actual wearing photographs support another question, an occasional *where/when* guide could compare that layer for an afternoon visit and a later dinner, showing what changes between activities. Its point would be the outfit decision, not a second retelling of the opening's design history.

If the packet has only a source-supported historical textile fact and no inspected garment, produce a culture explainer and a labelled diagram. If it has only current model facts, publish a product guide and perhaps a detail carousel. **Story count is determined by evidence, not the number of available channels.**

## Production and cadence assumptions

These are **planning estimates**, not observed Ocharo labour or promises made by Maftuna. Assume ops drafts and assembles, Maftuna reviews any brand or garment claim, existing photography is accessible, no commissioned film, no travel, and one English source article. New live shooting, travel, translation and full language review add time. A reply or approval delay is not counted as labour.

| Work on one verified story | Rough active time | Main dependency |
|---|---:|---|
| Locate evidence, select real images, check current claims | `1–2.5 h` | Product record, actual garment, source, rights |
| Edit one existing Blog draft into a sourced final story | `1–2 h` | Accurate notes and approved attribution |
| Build one four-to-six-frame carousel from selected images | `0.75–1.5 h` | Reviewable images, caption language |
| Review and correct brand/factual copy | `0.75–2 h` | Maftuna’s availability; translator if needed |
| **Minimal article + carousel package** | **`3.5–8 h`** | Excludes fresh shooting, travel, full translation |
| Add one Reel from footage already captured | `+1–3 h` | Motion worth showing, editing, caption review |
| Add an occasion/visual guide | `+1–2 h` initially | Wearing examples; verify any named venue, date, or dress code |
| Assemble one monthly email digest | `+0.5–1 h` after setup | Opt-in list, sender, delivery, approvals |

**Pilot proposal:** four evidence packets over eight weeks, at most two substantial Blog stories per month. Reuse existing Instagram slots *if* the separate three-post weekly mix is approved; do not add a fourth standing obligation. Let news and location updates interrupt the story cadence when genuinely time-sensitive. The estimated minimal package is `14–32` active hours over eight weeks before new shooting, travel, full translation, and email setup. If that load competes with merchant fulfilment, reduce to one packet per month and keep the product guide current. Lenny and TOAST publish at different scale; neither provides a viable staffing forecast for Ocharo.

**Language assumption:** English can serve international reading, while the domestic merchant/visitor-facing Instagram caption sequence proposed in `och-planning.md` is Uzbek first, Russian second, one English line. A full English article and an Uzbek-first social caption should be separately edited for their audiences, not mechanically sliced or machine-translated without review. If actual reader or merchant questions point elsewhere, adjust with evidence.

## What to measure in the pilot

The preview has no installed analytics, opt-in list, public search traffic, or observed conversion baseline. Counts below require publication and measurement setup; this report reports **zero measured outcomes**, not zero audience interest. Current social-account figures in planning were read on 2026-09-25 and are not refreshed here.

| Question | Evidence to record | Interpretation limit |
|---|---|---|
| Did the piece reach people? | Native post/reel reach and profile visits; Blog landing pages when publicly hosted | Reach is distribution, not useful reading or buying intent |
| Was the format useful? | Saves, sends/shares, replies with a concrete follow-up question; article-to-related or guide clicks | Public likes and comments alone are not comparable engagement rates |
| Could a visitor act? | Occasion-guide visits, current model clicks, optional verified stockist/contact clicks; inquiries naming a city or model | A click is not a visit or sale; record self-reported origin where possible |
| Did a buyer move forward? | Qualified merchant inquiries, requested product brief, samples, orders, reorders; source and city in the existing register | A merchant may see several touches; do not assign the whole order to one post |
| Did the system stay accurate? | Time since stockist/stock check, correction count, hours per packet, approvals delayed | Fast publishing with stale facts is a failed operating model |
| Did an email work, if launched? | Consented subscribers, delivered messages, article/location clicks, replies, unsubscribes | A list without a sending process is not an email channel |

The [Meta professional dashboard](https://about.fb.com/news/2024/10/best-practices-education-hub-creators-instagram/) describes native creation, engagement, and reach guidance. For a future public site, [Google’s measurement guidance](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/13566436?hl=en) shows how outbound link clicks can be detected; [Google’s lead-event reference](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/9267735?hl=en) distinguishes an inquiry from a qualified or converted lead. Those are **possible instruments**, not evidence that Ocharo has installed them. A simple manual source note in an inquiry register can begin earlier.

Compare formats only when they perform the **same reader job** and have comparable distribution. Review after eight weeks: which questions produced follow-up, which guides stayed current, how much verified production each story took, and whether any buyer progressed. Visible likes alone cannot support a claim that the Blog caused international sales.
